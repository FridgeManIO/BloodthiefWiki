* [Bloodthief Data by Gribovar](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1HdAnAZaL4W4uIpMkvH7w3az-70uQCw1-ZU3z57yp8dk/edit?gid=0#gid=0) - Spreadsheet including GK times, artifacts list, lore notes, etc
